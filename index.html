TRANSPARENCY

Our Methodology

How we collect, process, and score variety reputation data.

1. Data Collection

Floraputation continuously crawls over 40 public data sources including Instagram, Pinterest, Reddit, Amazon, specialist gardening forums (GardenWeb, UKGardening, etc.), and professional grower communities. Data is collected in real time and stored in a structured pipeline.

2. Natural Language Processing

Each mention is processed by our NLP engine, which performs entity recognition (identifying the variety name), sentiment classification (positive / neutral / negative), and audience segmentation (consumer, grower, or retailer). The engine supports 12 languages and is fine-tuned on horticultural vocabulary.

3. Reputation Score Calculation

The Reputation Score (0–100) is a weighted composite of:

  • Sentiment ratio (40%) — share of positive vs. negative mentions
  • Mention volume (25%) — total mentions relative to category average
  • Trend momentum (20%) — month-over-month change in mention volume
  • Audience breadth (15%) — diversity of consumer, grower, and retailer mentions

4. Strategic Decision Logic

DecisionCriteria
🚀 Push & ScaleScore ≥ 75 AND trend ≥ 0 AND negative sentiment < 20%
💰 Raise PriceConsumer score − Grower score ≥ 15 (demand exceeds supply difficulty)
🌿 Elite Growers OnlyConsumer score ≥ 80 AND Grower score ≤ 55 (high demand, high complexity)
🛑 Phase OutScore < 60 OR trend < −3% for 3+ consecutive months

5. Data Update Frequency

Scores are recalculated weekly. Trend data is available at 3-month, 6-month, 1-year, and 3-year intervals. Regional breakdowns are updated monthly.

6. Limitations & Transparency

Floraputation analyses publicly available online data only. Scores reflect online sentiment and do not capture offline sales data, trial results, or breeder proprietary information. Varieties with fewer than 100 public mentions are excluded from scoring to ensure statistical reliability.

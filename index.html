METHODOLOGY

How the Score is Built

A transparent, reproducible scoring system built for professional decision-making. Only varieties with 100+ verified mentions are included.

01
Data Ingestion

Continuous crawling of social media, e-commerce reviews, grower forums and industry media across 8 key markets. Only varieties with 100+ verified mentions are included.

02
NLP Processing

Aspect-based sentiment analysis separates consumer, grower and retailer voices. Mentions classified by sentiment, topic and source credibility.

03
Weighted Scoring

Industry media (1.5x), e-commerce reviews (1.3x), grower forums (1.1x), social media (1.0x). Recency decay applied over 30 days.

04
Live Updates

Scores recalculated every 6 hours. Rolling merge of new data with historical weighted scores for stability and accuracy.

Reputation Score Formula
Score = Σ(weighted_sentiment × source_weight × recency_decay) / total_mentions × confidence_factor
